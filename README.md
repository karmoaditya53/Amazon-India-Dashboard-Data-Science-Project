# 📊 Amazon India Sales Management Dashboard

An end-to-end executive performance management dashboard designed in Microsoft Excel for business leadership. This project translates 10,000+ e-commerce transactions across India into high-level KPI cards, interactive Pivot Tables, dynamic charts, and actionable strategic recommendations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview & Business Scenario
- **Objective:** Build an intuitive, executive-ready management dashboard for non-technical leadership (Ravi) to evaluate overall commercial health, category drivers, delivery pipelines, and geographic revenue concentration.
- **Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (Advanced Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Multi-Table Slicers, Calculated Fields, COUNTIFS, Dynamic Formatting).
- **Dataset:** 10,000 transaction records spanning multi-category orders from January 2024 to August 2026 across Indian states.

---

## 🚀 Key Performance Indicators (Executive Summary)
- **Gross Sales Revenue:** ₹15.58 Crore (₹155,789,894)
- **Net Operating Profit:** ₹3.32 Crore (₹33,167,008)
- **Net Profit Margin:** **21.29%**
- **Total Orders Logged:** 10,000 transactions
- **Total Units Sold:** 24,926 units (~2.49 units/order)
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹15,578.99

---

## 🔍 Analytical Objectives & Findings

### 1. Overall Sales & Profitability
- Delivered consistent operating margins averaging **21.29%** across the entire product catalog.
- High Average Order Value (₹15.58k) driven primarily by high-ticket consumer technology and smart electronics.

### 2. Category & Product Performance Leaderboard
- **Revenue Concentration:** **Electronics & Mobiles** drives **79.32% of total sales** (₹12.36 Cr) and ₹2.63 Cr in net profit.
- **Volume vs. Value Dichotomy:** **Apparel & Fashion** generated the 2nd highest volume (6,452 units across 2,580 orders), but contributed only 6.31% of revenue due to lower average selling price (ASP).
- **Top 5 SKUs:** `Laptop Backpack` (₹2.65 Cr), `Power Bank 20000mAh` (₹2.55 Cr), `5G Smartphone` (₹2.43 Cr), `Wireless Earbuds` (₹2.38 Cr), and `Smartwatch` (₹2.33 Cr) together generate over ₹12.35 Cr.
- **Untapped Growth:** **Pantry & Groceries** accounts for under 1% of revenue (₹15.27 Lakhs) despite healthy 21.59% margins, highlighting cross-selling opportunities.

### 3. Order Pipeline Status & Revenue Leakage
- **Healthy Pipeline:** **90.15%** of all orders are either fulfilled (**81.47% Delivered**, 8,147 orders) or actively in-transit (**8.68% Shipped**, 868 orders).
- **Revenue Loss:** **9.85% total leakage** (₹1.56 Crore lost across 985 unfulfilled orders):
  - **Cancellations (5.00%, 500 orders):** ₹77.75 Lakhs lost pre-fulfillment. Led by *Apparel & Fashion* (5.74% cancellation rate), indicating sizing uncertainty and customer hesitation.
  - **Returns (4.85%, 485 orders):** ₹75.77 Lakhs lost post-delivery. *Electronics & Mobiles* recorded a 5.39% return rate, driving **79.4% (₹1.24 Cr) of all monetary loss** due to high ticket values.

### 4. Channels, Logistics & Geographic Concentration
- **Payment Rails:** **UPI** is the dominant payment rail (**50.40% share**, ₹7.85 Cr), followed by Credit/Debit Cards (**19.94%**, ₹3.11 Cr) and Cash on Delivery (**14.83%**, ₹2.31 Cr).
- **Fulfillment Models:** **Amazon (FBA)** handles **69.02% of sales** (₹10.75 Cr across 7,054 orders). **Seller Flex** delivers the highest operating profit margin across channels (**21.84%**).
- **Top Geographic Markets:** Three states generate nearly half (**49.0%**) of all company revenue:
  1. **Maharashtra:** ₹2.96 Cr (18.97% share | 1,840 orders)
  2. **Karnataka:** ₹2.44 Cr (15.67% share | 1,515 orders)
  3. **Delhi:** ₹2.24 Cr (14.35% share | 1,444 orders)

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

| Focus Area | Strategic Action | Business Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High-Value Returns** | Mandate OTP verification on delivery and tamper-evident packaging for Electronics > ₹10,000. | Recovers an estimated ₹40–50L in return and damage claims. |
| **Apparel Cancellations** | Implement verified size-fit guides, visual customer reviews, and immediate dispatch alerts. | Reduces the 5.74% pre-dispatch cancellation rate. |
| **Cash on Delivery (COD)** | Offer a 1–2% instant checkout incentive for UPI/Amazon Pay Later. | Lowers courier RTO (Return to Origin) fees and cash handling friction. |
| **Logistics & Inventory** | Pre-position 50%+ of fast-moving FBA inventory within Western (Mumbai) and Southern (Bengaluru) fulfillment hubs. | Guarantees 24–48 hour delivery in core revenue corridors. |
| **Margin Expansion** | Scale vendor onboarding for the **Seller Flex** model. | Capitalizes on its superior 21.84% margin profile without compromising Prime delivery. |
